# Reference image generation

Mode: built-in image generation tool. Six independent full-body photorealistic references with transparent backgrounds, generated for this application. Images are bundled under assets/ and used by the app to derive white contours at runtime. No photos are fetched during use.

Shared prompt constraints: one adult woman; realistic studio photograph, natural skin and hands, soft diffused lighting, full head/hands/feet/accessories visible, transparent background, clear exterior edges, no text or watermark. Asset type: camera pose reference cutout.
